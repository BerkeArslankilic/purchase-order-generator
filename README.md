# Purchase Order Generator

I noticed that brands lose a ton of time creating purchase orders from scratch after a sale. I made this tool to fix that. 

You just type in the necessary information and it instantly generates a clean, printable purchase order. No servers, no sign-ups, just a straightforward tool that runs directly in your browser.

## How to use
Just open the site, fill in your details and the items, then hit generate. You can print it or save it as a PDF directly from the page.
